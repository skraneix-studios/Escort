Privacy Policy — Escort

Effective date: November 14, 2026

1. About this policy

Escort is a puzzle game for iOS, iPadOS, and Android, developed by Skraneix Studios (“we,” “us,” or “our”). This policy explains how information is handled when you use the game or contact us.

Escort has no in-app accounts. Gameplay, progress, and inventory are stored on your device. The current app includes no advertising, behavioral analytics, third-party crash-reporting SDK, or multiplayer service. Purchases use Apple’s App Store or Google Play directly; Escort does not use RevenueCat.

2. Information stored on your device

Escort stores information needed to operate the game, including:

  • Level completion, stars, route progress, and tutorial or onboarding status.
  • Coins, hints, blueprints, and earned rewards.
  • Ship ownership, selected ship and appearance, level reveal records, and assistance usage.
  • Sound, haptic, theme, accessibility-related game settings, and whether a review request has been made.
  • Cached purchase entitlements and transaction identifiers used to avoid granting a purchase twice.

3. Purchases and store services

Escort offers optional one-time purchases, including permanent ships or ship bundles and consumable supply bundles. The current game does not offer subscriptions. Apple or Google processes payments and may process account information, transaction history, payment details, device information, and other information under its own policies.

The app receives purchase information such as product identifiers, transaction identifiers, purchase status, and store-provided transaction or verification information to deliver purchases and support restoration. Relevant entitlement and transaction records are stored locally. We do not receive your full card number, bank details, or store account password through the app.

Eligible permanent purchases can be restored through the original store account, subject to store rules and availability. Restore Purchases covers eligible permanent purchases. Unused consumables and level progress may be lost if the device save is removed or does not transfer to a new device. Store transaction records may remain after uninstalling Escort.

4. Support messages and bug reports

If you choose to email us, we receive your email address, message, and any information or attachments you send, such as screenshots, device details, or purchase references. The app opens your email application; it does not silently send a report or upload game logs. Please avoid sending payment credentials or other unnecessary sensitive information.

We use correspondence to answer requests, investigate bugs, resolve purchase issues, and maintain our services. Our email service providers process messages to provide email delivery and storage. We retain correspondence for as long as reasonably needed for those purposes or applicable legal obligations. You can contact us to request deletion, subject to legitimate retention requirements.

5. Permissions and network activity

Escort does not request access to your contacts, precise location, camera, microphone, installed-app history, or advertising identifier for gameplay. Audio playback and haptics use operating-system facilities.

Gameplay operates locally. Network or external-service activity can occur when retrieving store products, purchasing or restoring items, using store review prompts, opening external links, sending an email through your email app, or using device backups. Those services may receive technical information such as your IP address and device details. Apple and Google may also provide developers with store sales reports and platform diagnostics under your platform settings and their policies. These are separate from an analytics or crash-reporting SDK embedded by us.

6. Sharing, advertising, and tracking

We do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. Escort does not include advertising SDKs, cross-app tracking, or tracking cookies. We may use service providers to handle support correspondence and may disclose relevant information when required by law or reasonably necessary to protect legal rights or address fraud. External websites and stores have their own data practices; this policy describes the Escort app.

7. Retention, backups, and deletion

Data from earlier test versions

If you used account or cloud-save features in an earlier version, contact Skraneix Studios to request deletion of that account and its online save. We may ask you to verify ownership. Please do not send passwords or payment details.

Local data remains until removed through available controls or by clearing app data or uninstalling, subject to operating-system behavior. Reset Progress clears saved stars and level completion; it does not erase all settings, inventory, earned entitlements, purchase records, or assistance history. To remove the local app container, use your device’s app-data deletion controls or delete the app. Offloading an app may preserve its data.

Apple, Google, or your device manufacturer may back up and restore some local data according to your settings. We do not access those backups. Manage backups separately if you want to remove retained copies. Deleting local data does not delete store purchase records or support emails already sent to us. Store records are retained under the store’s policies and legal obligations.

8. Your choices and privacy rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, portability, restriction, or objection concerning personal information we handle. Contact us using the details below. We may request information reasonably needed to verify a legacy account or support request. We cannot remotely delete data held only on your device. Apple and Google handle requests concerning their own independent systems.

9. Children’s privacy

Escort does not require players to submit personal information and does not include advertising or social messaging. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through gameplay. A parent or guardian who believes a child has provided personal information in a support message can contact us to request review or deletion. Purchases are subject to the platform’s parental controls and authorization settings.

10. Security and international processing

We use platform-provided private app storage and store services for payment processing. No storage or transmission method is completely secure. Protect your device and store accounts. Support providers and platform services may process information in additional countries under their practices and applicable law.

11. Third-party privacy policies

  • Apple Privacy Policy — App Store, purchases, reviews, device services, and backups.
  • Google Privacy Policy — Google Play, billing, reviews, device services, and backups.

12. Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the game or our practices change. The updated policy will show a revised effective date. Where appropriate or required, we will provide additional notice.
